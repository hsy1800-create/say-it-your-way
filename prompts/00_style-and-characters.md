# 00 · Style and characters (먼저 만들기)

모든 그림이 같은 그림책처럼 보이게 하는 공통 설정이다. 각 쪽 프롬프트에 이미 다 들어 있으니 따로 붙일 필요는 없다. 다만 **캐릭터 시트 4장을 먼저 만들어 두고**, 책마다 새 대화를 열 때 그 시트 이미지를 함께 올리면 얼굴이 훨씬 일정하게 나온다.

## Shared style

```text
Art style: a cute, cozy children's storybook illustration in the look of soft painted clipart. Smooth hand-painted shapes with gentle shading and a faint paper grain, rounded chubby proportions, no harsh black outlines (only soft darker edges of each color), and a warm pastel palette led by blush pink, heart red, cream, sage green and honey brown, with small touches of butter yellow and sky blue. Backgrounds stay light and simple. Little hearts, flowers and leaves may appear as gentle decorations. Faces are sweet and expressive, with simple dot eyes with a white highlight, rosy cheeks and small smiles. Adults are about 5 heads tall, children about 3.5 heads tall. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not anime, no glossy plastic look.
```

## Characters

```text
THE TEACHER (the narrator of the book): a cheerful Korean woman in her early 30s with long straight black hair falling past her shoulders to her upper chest, tucked behind one ear and held with a small yellow star-shaped hair clip, round thin black-framed glasses, a small warm smile, a soft blush-pink knit cardigan over a white T-shirt, sage-green straight-leg pants and white sneakers.
```

```text
THE TEACHER AS AN 8-YEAR-OLD GIRL (a childhood memory): black hair in a short chin-length bob held with the same small yellow star-shaped clip, round thin black-framed glasses, a yellow T-shirt with a small white star on it, denim shorts and brand-new pink jelly sandals.
```

```text
THE TEACHER AS A HIGH-SCHOOL STUDENT (a memory): the same face, round thin black-framed glasses and small yellow star-shaped clip, black hair in a low ponytail, wearing a Korean school uniform (navy blazer, white shirt, red ribbon tie, navy plaid skirt).
```

```text
GRANDMA: a small, sturdy Korean grandmother in her mid-70s with a short, tightly curled gray perm, warm crinkly eyes and rosy cheeks, wearing a blouse with small pink and purple flowers under a lavender knitted vest, loose dark-gray pants, and green rubber garden boots when outdoors.
```

```text
THE TEACHER'S CAT: a chubby gray tabby with darker gray stripes, a white chest and white paws, round golden-yellow eyes, a small pink nose, a fluffy tail and a slightly grumpy but secretly affectionate face.
```

```text
DAD (in the childhood memory, late 30s): tall and slim with short black hair, a kind smile, a light-blue striped short-sleeve shirt, khaki shorts and sunglasses pushed up on his head.
```

```text
MOM (in the childhood memory, late 30s): shoulder-length wavy black hair, a wide straw sun hat and a coral sundress, carrying a canvas beach bag.
```

```text
THE PUPPY: a fluffy, bouncy golden-retriever puppy with floppy ears, a big pink tongue and a wagging tail.
```
