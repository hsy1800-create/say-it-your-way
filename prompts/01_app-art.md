# 01 · 앱 꾸밈 그림 (선택)

앱 첫 화면에 들어가는 마스코트 그림이에요. 없어도 앱은 그대로 돌아가고, 넣으면 첫 화면 이름 옆에 나타나요.
보내주신 분홍 트럭 그림처럼 귀엽고 따뜻한 느낌으로 맞췄어요. 그 그림을 GPT에 **분위기 참고용**으로 함께 올려도 좋아요(똑같이 베끼지 말고 느낌만 맞춰 달라고 써 두었어요).

## 마스코트 → `art/mascot.png`

만든 그림은 저장소의 `public/art/mascot.png`로 넣어요. 배경이 투명하면 가장 예뻐요.

```text
Art style: a cute, cozy children's storybook illustration in the look of soft painted clipart. Smooth hand-painted shapes with gentle shading and a faint paper grain, rounded chubby proportions, no harsh black outlines (only soft darker edges of each color), and a warm pastel palette led by blush pink, heart red, cream, sage green and honey brown. If I uploaded a style reference image, match its mood, softness and colors, but do not copy its objects or layout.

Subject: the mascot for a children's English picture-book app. A small, chubby honey-brown teddy bear with round ears, rosy cheeks, a tiny smile and a light cream muzzle, wearing a little sage-green apron. The bear sits and happily hugs an open blush-pink picture book to its chest. Three glossy heart-shaped balloons in heart red and coral float above, their thin strings tied around the bear's wrist. A small cluster of cream and pink flowers with green leaves sits at the bear's feet.

Format: square, 1:1 (1024 x 1024). The bear fills about 70% of the image and is centered, with nothing cut off at the edges. Transparent background if possible, otherwise a plain pure white background with no shadow on the ground.

Important: absolutely no text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere, including on the book and the balloons.
```

## 다른 느낌이 필요할 때 덧붙이는 말

| 원하는 것 | 덧붙일 말 |
| --- | --- |
| 배경을 투명하게 | `Make the background transparent.` |
| 더 단순하게 | `Make it simpler, with fewer flowers and softer shading.` |
| 곰 대신 다른 동물 | `Change the teddy bear to a small white bunny, keeping everything else the same.` |
